Start it properly.

Your business isn't standard. Neither is what it needs. Startsheet AI gets your limited company ready: the papers, the books and the brand, in one launch pack.

What Startsheet does

  1. Company registration and founder agreement

    Prepares your registration details and drafts an agreement between the founders.

  2. Bookkeeping and first-year tax records

    Sets up your books and the records you keep through your first year.

  3. Logo and brand identity

    Designs your logo, colours and type, ready for your website and invoices.

How it works

  1. Tell Startsheet about your business and who is starting it.
  2. Startsheet AI prepares your launch pack.
  3. You check everything before you use it, and make the changes you want.

Questions

What is in the launch pack?
  • Your company registration details, ready to check
  • A draft founder agreement
  • Your bookkeeping set-up and first-year tax records
  • Your logo and brand identity
Is this legal or tax advice?

No. What Startsheet prepares is not legal or tax advice. Startsheet is not a solicitor or an accountant, and a draft is not a substitute for one. For anything with serious consequences you should take professional advice.

Is Startsheet connected to Companies House or HMRC?

No. Startsheet has no connection with either body and does not file anything for you. It prepares your registration details; registering the company stays with you.

Can I be sure my name and logo are available?

No. Startsheet does not clear names or logos. Check the Companies House register and the trade marks register yourself before you rely on them.

What happens to my details?

Nothing is collected here. Startsheet is currently a research prototype; see About this study.
